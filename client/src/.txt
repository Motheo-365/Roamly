I think the name and visual identity should make it feel like a real travel product, not a student CRUD project.

My pick: Roamly 🌍

Roamly — Plan less. Explore more.



It feels like an actual consumer app and fits the idea of trips, itineraries and budgeting without being too corporate.

Theme

I'd go for a modern travel-editorial aesthetic:



 Warm off-white background 

 Deep navy/charcoal text 

 One warm accent colour (terracotta/coral) 

 Large destination photography 

 Rounded cards, but not overly bubbly 

 Clean typography 

 Subtle map/route elements 

 Lots of whitespace
 The personality

Not:

✈️🌴🌈 FUN TRAVEL APP!!!

More:

Roamly
Your trips, thoughtfully planned.

It should feel like something a young professional would actually use.

Colour direction

I'd use something roughly like:

Background    #F7F4EF
Text          #1E2428
Primary       #D96C4A
Secondary     #6F7D75
Cards         #FFFFFF

The terracotta gives it a travel/adventure feeling without looking like every blue SaaS dashboard.

Logo

Very simple:

roamly

with a tiny curved route/compass element incorporated into the logo.

For example:

roamly 〰

or a small locatjion-pin/route mark beside the wordmark.
